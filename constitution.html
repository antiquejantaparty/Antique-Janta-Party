Constitution of the
Antique Janta Party

The supreme governing document of the Antique Janta Party, founded upon constitutional values, democratic principles, integrity, transparency and informed civic participation.

Read Constitution

Articles

Article I — Name & Identity

Section 1 — Name

The official name of the organisation shall be Antique Janta Party (AJP).

Section 2 — Motto

“कलम हमारी • आवाज आपकी”

Section 3 — Nature

Antique Janta Party shall function as an independent civic and political discussion organisation founded upon constitutional values, democratic participation, respectful dialogue and informed citizenship.

Section 4 — Official Language

English shall remain the principal working language of the organisation. Other languages may be used wherever appropriate.

Article II — Fundamental Principles

  • Respect for the Constitution of India.
  • Freedom of expression with responsibility.
  • Equality before every member.
  • No discrimination based upon religion, caste, language, ethnicity, gender or region.
  • Evidence-based discussion.
  • Transparency and accountability.
  • Democratic participation.
  • Independent thinking over blind political support.

Article III — Objectives

  • Promote constitutional awareness.
  • Encourage informed political discussion.
  • Strengthen democratic participation.
  • Provide a respectful platform for debate.
  • Reject misinformation through factual discussion.
  • Promote civic responsibility.
  • Maintain organisational neutrality.
  • Protect freedom of expression within constitutional limits.

Article IV — Membership

Eligibility

  • Accept this Constitution.
  • Follow organisational rules.
  • Maintain respectful conduct.
  • Support democratic and constitutional values.
  • Not have been permanently removed for serious misconduct.

Rights

  • Participate in organisational discussions.
  • Vote whenever eligible.
  • Contest eligible constitutional offices.
  • Submit suggestions and proposals.
  • Receive equal treatment without discrimination.

Duties

  • Respect fellow members.
  • Protect the reputation of AJP.
  • Maintain discipline.
  • Promote constructive discussion.
  • Follow this Constitution faithfully.

Article V — Constitutional Offices

The governing structure of Antique Janta Party shall consist of the following constitutional offices:

  1. President
  2. Vice-President
  3. Executive Director
  4. Deputy Chairperson
  5. Secretary-General
  6. Chief Whip
  7. Media Director
  8. Director of Administration

Additional constitutional offices may be created only through constitutional amendment whenever organisational requirements demand.

Article VI — President

The President shall be the Head of the Antique Janta Party and the highest constitutional authority responsible for preserving the vision, integrity and constitutional values of the organisation.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Provide overall leadership and strategic direction.
  • Represent AJP before the public.
  • Preside over constitutional meetings.
  • Approve major organisational decisions.
  • Safeguard this Constitution.
  • Maintain unity and organisational discipline.

Constitutional Protection

The office of the President shall remain constitutionally protected and cannot be removed through any ordinary vote, election or disciplinary process. The office shall only become vacant upon voluntary resignation or permanent inability to continue.

Article VII — Vice-President

The Vice-President shall serve as the second-highest constitutional authority and shall assist the President in carrying out the administration of AJP.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Assist the President in executive functions.
  • Act as President whenever required.
  • Coordinate constitutional activities.
  • Supervise organisational projects assigned by the President.
  • Promote unity and constitutional governance.

Constitutional Protection

The Vice-President shall remain constitutionally protected and may not be removed through any ordinary vote, election or disciplinary proceeding except by voluntary resignation or permanent inability to continue.

Article VIII — Executive Director

The Executive Director shall serve as the Chief Executive Officer of the organisation and shall supervise the implementation of policies and constitutional decisions.

Election

Elected by AJP members through a free, fair, and transparent vote held every 6 months. An Executive Director may serve only one consecutive term and must sit out the next term before becoming eligible to contest again. During this period, they may serve in another party office, such as Administration Director.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Execute decisions approved by the leadership.
  • Supervise day-to-day administration.
  • Coordinate departments.
  • Manage constitutional implementation.
  • Report organisational progress to the President and Vice-President.

Article IX — Appointment & Removal of Constitutional Officers

Appointment

Appointments to every constitutional office, except that of the President, Vice-President and Executive Director, shall require the approval of the President, the Vice-President and the Executive Director.

Removal

Any constitutional office below the Executive Director may be removed only upon receiving a two-thirds (2/3) majority decision of the following Constitutional Executive Council:

  • President
  • Vice-President
  • Executive Director

The decision of the Constitutional Executive Council shall be final and binding.

Neither the President nor the Vice-President shall be subject to removal under this Article.

Article X — Deputy Chairperson

The Deputy Chairperson shall assist the Executive Leadership in organisational coordination and constitutional administration.

  • Support executive meetings.
  • Coordinate internal affairs.
  • Assist constitutional administration.
  • Perform duties assigned by the Executive Leadership.

Article XI — Secretary-General

The Secretary-General shall serve as the principal administrative officer responsible for maintaining the official records of the organisation.

  • Maintain official documents.
  • Prepare meeting minutes.
  • Issue official notices.
  • Maintain constitutional records.
  • Coordinate internal communication.

Article XII — Chief Whip

The Chief Whip shall maintain discipline, order and constitutional compliance throughout the organisation.

  • Maintain organisational discipline.
  • Ensure adherence to the Constitution.
  • Report disciplinary matters.
  • Promote professionalism.
  • Assist in conflict resolution.

Article XIII — Media Director

The Media Director shall supervise the public communication, branding and official publications of the organisation.

  • Manage official announcements.
  • Supervise social media.
  • Protect organisational reputation.
  • Coordinate approved campaigns.
  • Ensure factual public communication.

Article XIV — Director of Administration

The Director of Administration shall supervise the administrative infrastructure, official platforms and operational management of the organisation.

  • Manage official community platforms.
  • Supervise administrative staff.
  • Maintain organisational security.
  • Protect organisational resources.
  • Implement administrative policies.

Article XV — Meetings

Regular Meetings

Regular meetings shall be conducted periodically to review organisational progress, discuss public affairs and strengthen coordination between constitutional offices.

Emergency Meetings

The President may convene an emergency meeting whenever immediate constitutional or administrative action becomes necessary.

Quorum

No constitutional decision shall be valid unless at least one-half of the eligible constitutional officers are present unless otherwise provided by this Constitution.

Article XVI — Elections

The Executive Director shall be elected through a free, fair and democratic vote conducted among eligible members.

  • Every eligible member shall possess one equal vote.
  • Voting shall remain confidential.
  • Election dates shall be officially announced.
  • The candidate securing the highest valid votes shall be declared elected.
  • Election procedures shall remain impartial and transparent.

Article XVII — Code of Conduct

  • Treat every member with dignity and respect.
  • No hate speech or harassment.
  • No discrimination.
  • No misinformation or deliberate propaganda.
  • Criticise ideas respectfully rather than attacking individuals.
  • Maintain professionalism on every official platform.
  • Respect organisational decisions.
  • Protect confidential information.

Article XVIII — Discipline

Every member shall maintain discipline, honesty and maturity while representing Antique Janta Party.

Disciplinary Measures

  • Verbal Warning
  • Written Warning
  • Temporary Suspension
  • Removal from Office
  • Termination of Membership

Every disciplinary proceeding shall follow fairness and natural justice before any final decision is taken.

Article XIX — Finance

The organisation may obtain resources through lawful voluntary contributions, approved fundraising activities and other transparent constitutional sources.

  • Financial records shall remain accurate.
  • Every expenditure shall be documented.
  • Financial transparency shall be maintained.
  • No funds shall be used contrary to the objectives of this Constitution.

Article XX — Constitutional Amendments

This Constitution may be amended whenever necessary to meet the evolving requirements of the organisation.

  • Any amendment may be proposed before the Executive Leadership.
  • A two-thirds majority shall be required for adoption.
  • An amendment shall become effective immediately unless otherwise specified.

Article XXI — Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution shall remain the supreme governing document of the Antique Janta Party.

Every office, regulation, decision, policy and administrative action shall remain subordinate to and consistent with this Constitution.

Oath of Membership

"I solemnly affirm that I shall faithfully uphold the Constitution of India and the Constitution of the Antique Janta Party; respect democratic values; promote truth, justice, equality and responsible public discourse; reject hatred and misinformation; discharge my duties with honesty, integrity and impartiality; and work towards building a disciplined, informed and constitutionally aware society."
