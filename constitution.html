Constitution of the Antique Janta Party

कलम हमारी • आवाज आपकी

Preamble

We, the members of the Antique Janta Party (AJP), united by our commitment to the Constitution of India, democratic values, respectful dialogue and informed citizenship, hereby adopt this Constitution to establish a disciplined, impartial and inclusive political discussion community.


AJP exists to promote constitutional awareness, encourage meaningful political discourse, protect freedom of expression within the limits of respectful conduct, inspire independent thinking over blind political support or blind political opposition, and strengthen democratic participation through informed public discussion.

Constitution

Article I — Name & Identity

Section 1. Name

The official name of the organisation shall be Antique Janta Party (AJP).

Section 2. Motto

"कलम हमारी • आवाज आपकी"

Section 3. Nature

AJP shall function as an independent civic and political discussion organisation committed to constitutional values, democratic participation and responsible public discourse.

Section 4. Official Language

The official working language shall be English. Additional languages may be used whenever appropriate.

Article II — Fundamental Principles

  • Respect for the Constitution of India.
  • Democracy above personality.
  • Independent thinking over blind political support.
  • Freedom of expression with responsibility.
  • Equality before law.
  • Evidence-based discussion.
  • Transparency and accountability.
  • Respectful democratic dialogue.
  • National unity beyond religion, caste, gender or region.

Article III — Objectives

  • Promote constitutional awareness.
  • Encourage respectful political discussion.
  • Develop informed citizens.
  • Strengthen democratic values.
  • Encourage critical thinking instead of blind political support.
  • Counter misinformation through facts and evidence.
  • Promote equality and justice.
  • Maintain an organised, transparent and disciplined community.

Article IV — Membership

Eligibility

  • Accepts this Constitution.
  • Supports democratic principles.
  • Agrees to follow Party regulations.
  • Maintains respectful conduct.
  • Has not been permanently removed for serious misconduct.

Rights

  • Participate in Party discussions.
  • Vote whenever eligible.
  • Contest constitutional positions where permitted.
  • Express opinions respectfully.
  • Appeal disciplinary decisions.

Duties

  • Respect fellow members.
  • Protect the reputation of AJP.
  • Follow constitutional provisions.
  • Maintain discipline.
  • Promote constructive discussion.

Article V — Organisational Structure

The constitutional leadership of the Antique Janta Party shall consist of:

  1. President
  2. Vice-President
  3. Executive Director
  4. Board Chairperson
  5. Deputy Chairperson
  6. Secretary-General
  7. Media Director
  8. Neutrality Guardian
  9. Session Scribe
  10. Chief Operations Officer (COO)

Additional constitutional offices may be established through constitutional amendment whenever necessary for the efficient functioning of the organisation.

Article VI — President

The President shall be the constitutional Head of the Antique Janta Party and the guardian of its vision, principles and constitutional integrity.

Constitutional Powers

  • Provide overall leadership to the organisation.
  • Safeguard the Constitution and Party principles.
  • Represent the organisation before the public.
  • Preside over constitutional meetings.
  • Exercise constitutional authority jointly with the Vice-President and Executive Director where required.
  • Ensure that all Party decisions remain consistent with this Constitution.

Tenure

The office of the President is a permanent constitutional office and shall not be removed through ordinary constitutional procedures.

Article VII — Vice-President

The Vice-President shall serve as the second-highest constitutional authority and shall assist the President in preserving the objectives and constitutional values of AJP.

Constitutional Powers

  • Assist the President in constitutional responsibilities.
  • Exercise presidential authority whenever authorised.
  • Participate in appointments and removals of constitutional office bearers.
  • Support the Executive Director in organisational administration.

Tenure

The office of the Vice-President is a permanent constitutional office and shall not be removed through ordinary constitutional procedures.

Article VIII — Executive Director

The Executive Director shall be the chief executive authority responsible for implementing organisational policies and supervising the day-to-day administration of AJP.

Election

The Executive Director shall be elected through a democratic vote conducted among eligible Party members.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Implement constitutional decisions.
  • Coordinate organisational departments.
  • Supervise administrative affairs.
  • Lead executive projects.
  • Recommend reforms for organisational improvement.

Appointment & Removal of Officials

The President, Vice-President and Executive Director shall jointly possess the constitutional authority to appoint or remove all remaining constitutional office bearers.

Any such appointment or removal shall require the approval of not less than two-thirds (2/3) of these three constitutional authorities.

Article IX — Board Chairperson & Deputy Chairperson

The Board Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson shall assist the constitutional leadership by maintaining organisational stability, advising on governance matters and strengthening institutional coordination.

  • Provide constitutional guidance.
  • Assist during executive meetings.
  • Support organisational development.
  • Carry out responsibilities assigned by the constitutional leadership.

Article X — Secretary-General

The Secretary-General shall serve as the principal administrative officer responsible for maintaining official records and ensuring effective internal communication.

  • Maintain constitutional records.
  • Prepare meeting agendas and minutes.
  • Issue official notices.
  • Maintain Party documentation.
  • Coordinate communication between leadership and members.

Article XI — Executive Offices

The following constitutional offices shall function under the authority of the Executive Leadership:

Media Director

  • Manage official communications.
  • Supervise public relations and digital media.
  • Protect the Party's public image.

Neutrality Guardian

  • Ensure political neutrality during internal proceedings.
  • Monitor fairness in discussions and decisions.
  • Report constitutional violations impartially.

Session Scribe

  • Record proceedings of official meetings.
  • Maintain archives of resolutions and debates.
  • Preserve official constitutional records.

Chief Operations Officer (COO)

  • Supervise administrative operations.
  • Manage official platforms and organisational infrastructure.
  • Coordinate moderators and administrative staff.
  • Maintain operational efficiency and security.

Article XII — Executive Leadership Council

The Executive Leadership Council shall be the principal governing body responsible for overseeing the administration, constitutional compliance and long-term development of the Antique Janta Party.

Functions

  • Develop organisational policies.
  • Approve strategic initiatives.
  • Review constitutional matters.
  • Supervise implementation of Party programmes.
  • Protect the integrity and stability of the organisation.

Article XIII — Meetings

Regular Meetings

Regular meetings shall be conducted periodically as determined by the Executive Leadership to review organisational progress, discuss public affairs and evaluate future initiatives.

Emergency Meetings

The President or Executive Director may convene an emergency meeting whenever immediate constitutional or administrative action becomes necessary.

Quorum

No constitutional decision shall be valid unless at least one-half of the eligible Executive Leadership is present unless otherwise provided under this Constitution.

Article XIV — Elections

Executive Director Election

The Executive Director shall be elected through a free, fair and transparent election conducted among eligible Party members.

Voting Principles

  • Every eligible member shall possess one vote.
  • Voting shall remain confidential unless otherwise announced.
  • The candidate receiving the highest valid votes shall be declared elected.
  • The Election Authority shall ensure impartiality throughout the electoral process.

Article XV — Appointments & Removal of Office Bearers

Except for the offices of President, Vice-President and Executive Director, every constitutional office shall be filled through appointment.

Appointment Authority

Appointments shall require the approval of at least two of the following constitutional authorities:

  • President
  • Vice-President
  • Executive Director

Removal

The removal of any appointed office bearer shall likewise require a two-thirds (2/3) decision of the President, Vice-President and Executive Director.

Article XVI — Code of Conduct

Every member of the Antique Janta Party shall uphold the dignity of the organisation by observing the following principles:

  • Respect every individual regardless of religion, caste, ethnicity, language, gender or region.
  • Maintain civil and respectful discussion.
  • Criticise policies and ideas rather than individuals.
  • Reject blind political support and blind political hatred.
  • Avoid misinformation and deliberate falsehoods.
  • Encourage fact-based discussion.
  • Protect confidential Party information.
  • Maintain professionalism on all official platforms.

Article XVII — Discipline

Discipline shall remain essential to the effective functioning of the Antique Janta Party.

Disciplinary Measures

  • Official Warning.
  • Temporary Suspension.
  • Removal from Constitutional Office.
  • Permanent Termination of Membership.

No member shall be permanently removed without being given an opportunity to present a defence except in extraordinary circumstances involving severe misconduct.

Article XVIII — Finance & Transparency

The Party may receive lawful financial support through voluntary contributions, fundraising activities and other approved sources.

  • Maintain complete financial records.
  • Ensure transparency wherever applicable.
  • Prevent misuse of Party resources.
  • Use organisational funds solely for constitutional objectives.

Article XIX — Constitutional Amendments

This Constitution may be amended whenever necessary to meet the evolving needs of the organisation.

  • Any amendment may be proposed before the Executive Leadership.
  • A two-thirds majority shall be required for approval.
  • Approved amendments shall become effective immediately unless otherwise stated.

Article XX — Dissolution

The Antique Janta Party shall only be dissolved through an extraordinary constitutional resolution supported by not less than three-fourths of the Executive Leadership after consultation with the membership.

Article XXI — Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution shall remain the supreme governing document of the Antique Janta Party.

Every regulation, policy, appointment and administrative action undertaken by the organisation shall remain consistent with its provisions.

Oath of Membership

"I solemnly affirm that I shall uphold the Constitution of India and the Constitution of the Antique Janta Party; respect democratic values; protect freedom of expression with responsibility; reject discrimination, hatred and misinformation; perform my duties honestly, impartially and with integrity; and faithfully serve the ideals, objectives and constitutional principles of the Antique Janta Party."
