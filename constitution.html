Constitution of the Antique Janta Party

कलम हमारी • आवाज आपकी

Preamble

We, the members of the Antique Janta Party (AJP), united by our commitment to the Constitution of India, democratic values, respectful dialogue and informed citizenship, hereby adopt this Constitution to establish a disciplined, impartial and inclusive political discussion community.


AJP exists to promote constitutional awareness, encourage meaningful political discourse, protect freedom of expression within the limits of respectful conduct, and inspire independent thinking over blind political support or hatred.

Constitution

Article I — Name & Identity

Section 1. Name

The official name of the organisation shall be Antique Janta Party (AJP).

Section 2. Motto

"कलम हमारी • आवाज आपकी"

Section 3. Nature

AJP shall function as an independent political discussion and civic awareness community committed to constitutional values, informed citizenship and respectful democratic dialogue.

Section 4. Official Language

The primary working language of AJP shall be English. Other languages may be used where appropriate.

Article II — Fundamental Principles

The following principles shall guide all activities of AJP:

  • Respect for the Constitution of India.
  • Freedom of expression with responsibility.
  • Equality and non-discrimination.
  • Independent thinking over blind support.
  • Evidence-based discussion.
  • Transparency and accountability.
  • Respectful democratic dialogue.
  • National unity and constitutional values.

Article III — Objectives

The objectives of Antique Janta Party shall be:

  • Promote informed political discussion and constitutional awareness.
  • Encourage respectful debate among individuals with diverse viewpoints.
  • Strengthen democratic participation and civic responsibility.
  • Combat misinformation through evidence-based discussion.
  • Encourage critical thinking instead of blind political support or hatred.
  • Provide a safe platform for constructive public discourse.
  • Promote mutual respect regardless of religion, caste, language, region, gender or ideology.
  • Build a disciplined, transparent and inclusive community.

Article IV — Membership

Section 1. Eligibility

Any individual may become a member of AJP provided that he or she:

  • Accepts this Constitution.
  • Agrees to follow community rules.
  • Supports democratic and constitutional values.
  • Maintains respectful conduct.
  • Has not been permanently removed for serious misconduct.

Section 2. Rights of Members

  • Participate in discussions and activities.
  • Express opinions respectfully.
  • Propose suggestions and initiatives.
  • Seek clarification regarding administrative actions.
  • Contest internal positions where eligible.

Section 3. Duties of Members

  • Respect fellow members.
  • Maintain discipline.
  • Follow community guidelines.
  • Protect the reputation of AJP.
  • Promote constructive and informed discussion.

Article V — Organisational Structure

The governing structure of the Antique Janta Party (AJP) shall consist of the following constitutional offices:

  1. President
  2. Vice-President
  3. Executive Director
  4. Deputy Chairperson
  5. Secretary-General
  6. Chief Whip
  7. Media Head
  8. Chief Admin

Additional constitutional offices may be established by amendment whenever necessary for the effective functioning of the organisation.

Article VI — President

The President shall be the Head of the Antique Janta Party and shall safeguard its vision, constitutional values and organisational integrity.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Provide overall leadership and direction.
  • Represent the organisation publicly.
  • Preside over constitutional meetings.
  • Approve major organisational decisions.
  • Ensure that every action remains consistent with this Constitution.
  • Promote unity, discipline and democratic values.

Article VII — Vice-President

The Vice-President shall serve as the second-highest constitutional office and shall assist the President in carrying out the objectives of AJP.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Assist the President in executive responsibilities.
  • Act on behalf of the President during absence or incapacity.
  • Coordinate organisational activities assigned by the President.
  • Support constitutional governance and internal administration.

Article VIII — Executive Director

The Executive Director shall supervise the execution of organisational policies and ensure efficient administration of AJP.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Implement decisions approved by the leadership.
  • Coordinate major organisational projects.
  • Oversee day-to-day administrative functions.
  • Monitor organisational performance and recommend improvements.
  • Ensure effective coordination between departments.

Article IX — Deputy Chairperson

The Deputy Chairperson shall assist the senior leadership in organisational affairs and provide guidance in constitutional matters.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Support the President and Vice-President.
  • Coordinate internal organisational affairs.
  • Assist during executive meetings.
  • Promote discipline and institutional stability.
  • Perform duties assigned by the President.

Article X — Secretary-General

The Secretary-General shall serve as the principal administrative officer responsible for official documentation and internal communication.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Maintain official records.
  • Prepare meeting agendas and minutes.
  • Issue official notices.
  • Maintain constitutional documents.
  • Coordinate communication between leadership and members.

Article XI — Chief Whip

The Chief Whip shall be responsible for maintaining discipline, order and adherence to the Constitution and community guidelines within the organisation.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Promote discipline and respectful conduct among members.
  • Ensure compliance with constitutional provisions and Party rules.
  • Assist in resolving internal disagreements through fair and impartial means.
  • Report serious disciplinary matters to the President and Executive Leadership.
  • Encourage unity, professionalism and constructive participation.

Article XII — Media Head

The Media Head shall oversee the public communication of AJP and ensure that all official information reflects the organisation's constitutional principles and objectives.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Manage official announcements and publications.
  • Supervise social media and digital communication.
  • Protect the public image of the organisation.
  • Ensure accuracy and neutrality in official statements.
  • Coordinate media campaigns approved by the leadership.

Article XIII — Chief Admin

The Chief Admin shall oversee the administration of the Party's official platforms and ensure their secure, organised and efficient operation.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Manage official community platforms and infrastructure.
  • Supervise moderators and administrative staff.
  • Maintain security and operational stability.
  • Implement administrative policies approved by the leadership.
  • Protect organisational resources and records.

Article XIV — Meetings

Regular Meetings

Regular meetings shall be conducted at intervals determined by the Executive Leadership to review organisational progress, discuss public affairs and strengthen internal coordination.

Emergency Meetings

The President may convene an emergency meeting whenever circumstances require immediate constitutional or administrative action.

Quorum

No constitutional decision shall be considered valid unless at least one-half of the eligible leadership members are present, unless otherwise provided by this Constitution.

Article XV — Elections

  • Leadership appointments or elections shall be conducted fairly and transparently.
  • Every eligible member shall have equal voting rights whenever elections are held.
  • Election procedures shall be announced before the commencement of voting.
  • The Executive Leadership shall ensure the integrity of every electoral process.

Article XVI — Code of Conduct

Every member of Antique Janta Party shall uphold the dignity of the organisation by adhering to the following principles:

  • Treat every individual with dignity and respect.
  • No discrimination on the basis of religion, caste, language, ethnicity, gender or region.
  • No hate speech or personal attacks.
  • No deliberate spread of misinformation.
  • Criticise ideas and policies rather than individuals.
  • Encourage evidence-based discussion.
  • Respect differing political opinions while maintaining civility.
  • Protect confidential organisational information.
  • Maintain professionalism on all official platforms.

Article XVII — Discipline

Discipline is fundamental to the integrity and effectiveness of the Antique Janta Party. Every member shall conduct themselves with honesty, maturity and respect.

Disciplinary Measures

  • Verbal or written warning.
  • Temporary suspension from organisational activities.
  • Removal from an office or responsibility.
  • Permanent termination of membership in cases of severe misconduct.

No member shall be permanently removed without being given an opportunity to present their explanation, except in extraordinary circumstances involving serious violations.

Article XVIII — Finance

The organisation may obtain resources through lawful and transparent means including voluntary contributions, fundraising activities and other approved sources.

  • All financial records shall be maintained accurately.
  • Any expenditure shall be properly documented.
  • Financial transparency shall be maintained whenever applicable.
  • No funds shall be used against the objectives of this Constitution.

Article XIX — Constitutional Amendments

This Constitution may be amended whenever necessary to meet the evolving needs of the organisation.

  • Any amendment proposal may be submitted before the Executive Leadership.
  • A two-thirds majority of the Executive Leadership shall be required for adoption.
  • Every amendment shall become effective immediately upon approval unless otherwise specified.

Article XX — Dissolution

The Antique Janta Party shall only be dissolved by an extraordinary resolution supported by not less than three-fourths of the Executive Leadership after consultation with the membership.

Article XXI — Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution shall remain the supreme governing document of the Antique Janta Party.

Every policy, decision, regulation and administrative action undertaken by the organisation shall remain consistent with the provisions contained herein.

Oath of Membership

"I solemnly affirm that I shall uphold the Constitution of India and the Constitution of the Antique Janta Party; respect democratic values; promote truth, justice, equality and respectful dialogue; reject blind political support and misinformation; perform my duties with honesty, integrity and impartiality; and faithfully work towards building an informed, united and constitutionally aware society."
