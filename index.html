Who We Are

Antique Association is a community-driven organisation that brings people together through sports, culture, social service and community activities.

◆

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage participation, promote community welfare and create opportunities for people to connect, contribute and grow.

✧

Our Foundation

Built on unity, respect, responsibility, teamwork, service and the belief that everyone can contribute to a stronger community.

Bringing people together regardless of background and encouraging cooperation, friendship and mutual respect.

02

Service

Encouraging members to participate in social welfare, blood donation and community service programmes.

Creating opportunities for members to actively participate in sports, culture, events and community programmes.

04

Supporting personal development, teamwork, leadership and a positive environment for young people.
