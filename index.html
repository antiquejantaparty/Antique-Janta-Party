WHO WE ARE

More Than An Association.

Antique Association exists to create a place where people can come together, participate, contribute and grow.

We organize and support sports activities, cultural celebrations, community programs, blood donation camps, awareness campaigns, educational initiatives and events that bring people closer.

Our strength is not one person. It is the collective energy of every member, volunteer, organizer, supporter and friend who believes that a better community is something we build together.

FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Antique Association is a community organization focused on sports, culture, social service, youth participation, education and community development.

Anyone who supports the association's community-focused values and wants to participate in its activities can apply for membership.

Activities can include sports tournaments, cultural programs, blood donation camps, awareness drives, workshops, community gatherings and youth initiatives.

Volunteer opportunities can be made available for specific events and community programs depending on the requirements of each initiative.
