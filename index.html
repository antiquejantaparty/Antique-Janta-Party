About Antique Janta Party

Antique Janta Party is not a political party. We are an independent Indian political discussion community where people can exchange ideas, debate respectfully and discuss national issues through the lens of constitutional values. We neither blindly support nor blindly oppose any political party. Our focus remains on facts, democratic principles, informed citizenship and meaningful dialogue.
