About Antique Association

Antique Association is a community-driven organisation built on the principles of service, fellowship, leadership and responsibility. It brings together individuals who believe that meaningful change begins when people work together.


The Association provides a platform for members to participate in social initiatives, community programmes, leadership activities and projects designed to create lasting positive impact.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To unite people through service, encourage responsible leadership and support initiatives that improve communities and strengthen society.

Our Vision

To build a respected and united association where every member has the opportunity to serve, lead, contribute and make a meaningful difference.

Our Core Principles

Service

We place community service and helping others at the heart of everything we do.

Fellowship

We create strong connections between members through cooperation, friendship and mutual respect.

Leadership

We encourage members to develop leadership, responsibility and decision-making skills.

Integrity

We believe that honesty, transparency and accountability must guide every action.

Association Leadership

President

Leads the Association and provides overall direction, coordination and representation.

Vice President

Supports the President and assists in coordinating Association activities and programmes.

Secretary-General

Manages official records, administration, communication and organisational coordination.

Parliamentary Leader

Coordinates parliamentary affairs and represents the Association in formal deliberations.

Chief Whip

Maintains coordination and discipline among Association representatives and members.

Chairperson of Policy & Strategy

Leads policy development, strategic planning and long-term organisational initiatives.

Treasurer-General

Oversees financial administration, budgeting and responsible management of Association funds.

Secretary of Public Affairs & Outreach

Manages public relations, outreach programmes and communication with the wider community.
