INDIA'S UNSUNG GEMS

Stories Worth Remembering

India has produced remarkable individuals whose contributions have shaped science, society and the world. Their stories deserve to be remembered.

01
SCIENCE • NOBEL LAUREATE

Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar

The Indian-born astrophysicist who transformed our understanding of stars

Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar was an Indian-born astrophysicist whose pioneering work on the structure and evolution of stars became one of the foundations of modern astrophysics.

Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar was born on 19 October 1910 in Lahore, then part of British India.

He became one of the world's leading theoretical astrophysicists. One of his most famous contributions was his calculation of the maximum mass that a stable white dwarf star can have.

This limit became known as the Chandrasekhar Limit. It is approximately 1.4 times the mass of the Sun.

His work helped explain how stars evolve and what can happen to them after they exhaust their nuclear fuel.

In 1983, Chandrasekhar was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics for his theoretical studies of the physical processes important to the structure and evolution of stars.

He was the second Indian-born Nobel laureate in a scientific field, following C. V. Raman, who received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930.

His legacy continues in modern astronomy, astrophysics and the study of stellar evolution.

02
SCIENCE • PHYSICS

Satyendra Nath Bose

A pioneer of quantum statistics

Satyendra Nath Bose was an Indian physicist whose work became a fundamental part of modern quantum mechanics.

Bose made groundbreaking contributions to quantum statistics.

His work was recognised by Albert Einstein, who translated and extended Bose's ideas.

The class of particles known as bosons was named after him.

Bose-Einstein statistics became one of the important foundations of quantum physics.

03
SPACE • SCIENCE

Vikram Sarabhai

The father of India's space programme

Vikram Sarabhai was a visionary physicist and institution builder who played a central role in establishing India's space programme.

Vikram Sarabhai believed that advanced science and technology could be used to solve real problems faced by society.

He played a foundational role in the development of India's space programme.

His vision contributed to the establishment and development of major Indian scientific institutions.

His legacy remains deeply connected with India's journey in space science and technology.

04
MEDICINE • BIOCHEMISTRY

Yellapragada Subbarow

A pioneering Indian biochemist

Yellapragada Subbarow was an Indian-born biochemist whose research contributed to important developments in medicine and biochemistry.

Yellapragada Subbarow carried out important research in biochemistry after moving to the United States.

His scientific work contributed to a deeper understanding of biochemical processes and helped influence the development of important medical treatments.

His career represents the contribution of Indian scientists whose achievements were sometimes less widely known to the public.

05
AEROSPACE • INDIA

Satish Dhawan

A key figure in India's aerospace journey

Satish Dhawan was an Indian aerospace engineer and scientist who played an important role in strengthening India's space programme.

Satish Dhawan was a distinguished aerospace engineer and scientist.

He became Chairman of the Indian Space Research Organisation and played a major role in strengthening India's capabilities in space research and engineering.

His leadership helped establish a strong scientific and engineering culture within India's space programme.

His contribution remains an important part of the history of Indian space exploration.
